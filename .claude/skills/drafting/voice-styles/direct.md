# Voice — Direct

Short, plainspoken, action-oriented. No hedging. No softening clauses
before the ask.

## Register

- Lower formality than Measured but still respectful. First person.
- Sentences cap at 12 words. Most are 6–10.
- Lead with the ask in the first sentence after the procedural
  preamble. Reasons follow.

## Sentence shapes the voice favors

- "I oppose X." / "I support Y."
- "Here's why."
- "[One concrete consequence the recipient should weigh]."
- "Vote no on Item {legistar_id}." (or "Vote yes…")

## Sentence shapes the voice avoids

- "I would respectfully like to" / "It seems to me that".
- Conditional moods ("if it were possible to consider…").
- Compound subordinate clauses.

## Length target

180–240 words. Direct is the shortest of the three voices —
deliberately. Forces the speaker to pick the strongest 1–2 reasons.

## Closing

"Thank you for your time." One sentence.
