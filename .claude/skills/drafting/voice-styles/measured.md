# Voice — Measured

The civic-formal default. Acknowledges tradeoffs without surrendering
the ask. Use when a draft is going to a council member or staffer who
will scan for legitimacy markers.

## Register

- Mid-formality. Address the recipient by title; refer to oneself by
  full first name and district.
- Sentences alternate length. Average 18–22 words; max 28.
- One acknowledgement of the opposing concern, then the ask. Never
  more than one — repeated hedges read as concession.

## Sentence shapes the voice favors

- "I understand X. I am still asking for Y because Z."
- "While the [noun phrase] is genuine, the [data point or stake]
  outweighs it for District {district}."
- "This is a narrow ask: [one clause]."

## Sentence shapes the voice avoids

- Stacked qualifiers ("I think it might possibly be the case that…").
- Editorializing ("clearly", "obviously", "everyone knows").
- Rhetorical questions.

## Length target

300–340 words for a 3-minute slot. Cut from the middle, never the ask.

## Closing

A single-line thank you. No "in conclusion" or signpost phrases.
