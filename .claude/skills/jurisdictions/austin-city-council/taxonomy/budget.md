# taxonomy/budget

Use when classifying items that set or change the aggregate financial
posture of the city: tax rate, annual budget, fee schedules, large grant
acceptances, debt.

## In scope

- Annual budget adoption and mid-year amendments.
- Property tax rate setting and homestead exemption changes.
- Fee schedules: utility rates (Austin Water, Austin Energy), development
  review fees, parks fees, EMS transport fees.
- Franchise agreements (Austin Energy, gas, cable).
- Grant acceptances ≥ $1M, especially with a required city match.
- Bond issuance, certificates of obligation, debt refinancing.
- General-fund reserves and inter-fund transfers.

## Out of scope

- Program-specific spending where the council vote is about the program's
  *shape* (e.g., new shelter siting) → tag the program's topic instead.
  Budget here is the aggregate and rate level.
- Small grant acceptances (< $1M, no match) → usually tag the program's
  topic alone.

## Signal words

`tax rate`, `adopt budget`, `amend FY`, `fee schedule`, `franchise`,
`general fund`, `CIP`, `capital improvement`, `homestead exemption`,
`grant acceptance`, `bond`, `certificate of obligation`, `CO issuance`,
`reserve`, `millage`, `rate schedule`.

## Examples

- "Adopt tax rate of $0.4430 per $100 assessed valuation" → `budget`.
- "Amend FY26 Austin Transportation budget to accept TxDOT grant" →
  `budget` + `transportation` (the transportation purpose is what a
  resident cares about; budget is the vote shape).
- "Adopt FY27 Austin Water rate schedule" → `budget`.
- "Approve FY27–FY29 certificates of obligation for CIP funding" →
  `budget`.
