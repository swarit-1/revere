# taxonomy/land-use

Use when classifying items about the physical and environmental rules
that shape Austin's land: parks, code, ecology, preservation.

## In scope

- Parkland dedication, park improvements, parkland acquisition.
- Historic preservation: landmark designation, historic-district rules,
  historic-zoning overlays (HD-*).
- Land Development Code (LDC) amendments: citywide setbacks, compatibility
  standards, impervious-cover rules, lot-size minimums.
- Watershed and environmental protection: critical water quality zones,
  water-quality transition zones, tree ordinance (Heritage Tree rules).
- Easements and right-of-way vacations that aren't transportation-focused.
- Floodplain and drainage code.

## Out of scope

- Residential rezonings (SF-*, MF-* changes) → `housing`.
- Commercial-only zoning overlays (CS-*, GR-*, LR-*) → `commercial-regulation`.
- Street geometry, sidewalks, and ROW vacations for mobility purposes →
  `transportation`.

## Signal words

`parkland`, `historic`, `LDC`, `Land Development Code`, `setback`,
`compatibility`, `impervious`, `watershed`, `critical water quality`,
`tree ordinance`, `Heritage Tree`, `landmark`, `easement`, `right-of-way
vacation`, `floodplain`, `drainage`, `HD-`.

## Examples

- "Designate 1600 Guadalupe as a City of Austin Historic Landmark" →
  `land-use`.
- "Amend LDC Subchapter C Article 10 compatibility standards" →
  `land-use`.
- "Accept donation of 5.3-acre parkland tract in District 3" →
  `land-use`.
- "Amend Heritage Tree ordinance to reduce mitigation ratios" →
  `land-use`.
