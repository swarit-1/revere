# taxonomy/public-safety

Use when classifying items about policing, emergency response, or
community safety.

## In scope

- Austin Police Department (APD): meet-and-confer contract, staffing,
  hiring, academy, discipline.
- Office of Police Oversight: funding, authority, case review procedure.
- Fire department and EMS: staffing, station siting, response-time policy.
- Emergency management: disaster declarations, winter-storm readiness,
  shelter activation.
- Body-worn camera policy, use-of-force rules, chokehold / lethal-force
  ordinances.
- 911 and public-safety communications.

## Out of scope

- Traffic-signal hardware or signal-timing policy → `transportation`.
- Fire-code amendments as a building-code matter → `land-use`.
- Liquor-license overlays where the concern is alcohol availability →
  `commercial-regulation`.
- Domestic-violence *service* funding where the vote is purely fiscal →
  `budget`; otherwise keep here.

## Signal words

`APD`, `police`, `oversight`, `use of force`, `body-worn`, `body cam`,
`fire department`, `AFD`, `EMS`, `emergency management`, `911`,
`public safety`, `chokehold`, `lethal force`, `consent decree`, `interim
chief`, `academy cadet`, `meet and confer`.

## Examples

- "Approve APD meet-and-confer agreement through FY28" → `public-safety`.
- "Create Office of Police Oversight with subpoena authority" →
  `public-safety`.
- "Accept FY26 federal COPS Hiring grant with 25% city match" →
  `public-safety` + `budget` (grant + match over $1M triggers the budget
  co-tag).
