# taxonomy/transportation

Use when classifying items about how people and goods move through Austin.

## In scope

- Transit: CapMetro coordination, bus-lane ordinances, Project Connect,
  MetroRail integration.
- Streets: repaving, traffic-signal technology, TxDOT grants, pavement
  condition reporting.
- Sidewalks and bikeways: construction, ADA compliance, shared-use paths.
- Parking: curb-use regulations, parking minimums/maximums, residential
  permit zones, EV charging in the right-of-way.
- Micromobility: e-scooters, bike share, delivery robots on sidewalks.
- Airport ground-side transport (ride-share pickup, transit access, taxi
  permitting).

## Out of scope

- Traffic enforcement as a police discipline → `public-safety`.
- Airside airport operations (runway, aviation safety) → rarely
  personas-relevant; skip or flag.
- Sidewalk cafés on commercial strips → `commercial-regulation`.
- LDC-wide street-geometry code changes → `land-use`.

## Signal words

`transit`, `MetroRail`, `CapMetro`, `Project Connect`, `signal`, `TxDOT`,
`sidewalk`, `bikeway`, `bike lane`, `scooter`, `micromobility`, `parking`,
`repave`, `pavement`, `ADA`, `ride-share`, `mobility`, `curb management`,
`residential permit parking`.

## Examples

- "Accept $7.9M TxDOT grant for advanced traffic signal vehicle detection"
  → `transportation` (primary) + `budget` (grant acceptance over $1M).
- "Authorize execution of CapMetro Project Connect interlocal agreement"
  → `transportation`.
- "Amend parking minimums in LDC Subchapter 25-6" → `transportation`
  (parking policy is the intent) + `land-use` (mechanism is an LDC
  amendment).
